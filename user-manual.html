I lost something

Four steps from missing to returned.

1

Search for your item

Type the item's name or its ASKI ID (e.g. "ASKI-000123") into the search bar on the Find page. Suggestions appear as you type — click one to jump straight to the item. You can also filter by category and status.

2

Check the details

Open the item page and read the full description: category, where it was found, the date, and any extra notes. Make sure it's really yours before claiming.

3

Submit a claim

Click "Claim this item" and fill out the claim form: your name, email, student ID, and details only the owner would know — where you last had it, unique marks, contents.

4

Get verified and pick it up

The SSG and EagleCatch team review claims within 24 hours. If yours matches, you'll be contacted at the email you provided to arrange pickup.

I found something

Thank you — here's how to hand it back.

1

Report it online

Open the Report Found Item form and describe the item: name, category, condition, exactly where you found it and when. The more detail, the faster the owner is found.

2

Turn it in

Hand the item to the SSG or EagleCatch desk so it can be kept safe until the owner comes forward. Please don't keep found property, even briefly.

3

We list it

The team verifies your report and posts the item on the Find board with a unique ASKI ID, usually within 24 hours.

4

The owner claims it

When someone submits a matching claim, the SSG verifies it and returns the item. You just made one more happy ASKIAn.

What item statuses mean

Every item on the board carries one of three badges.

Available

Verified and ready to be claimed. Submit a claim and the team will check it within 24 hours.

Found

Recently turned in and still being processed. It becomes Available once verification is complete.

Claimed

Already returned to its owner. If you believe an item was claimed in error, contact the team with proof of ownership.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to get my item back?

Nothing. EagleCatch is a free service run by the SSG. If anyone asks for payment in exchange for a returned item, report it to the SSG office immediately.

How long do items stay on the board?

Items stay listed until they're claimed or the semester ends. Unclaimed items are turned over to the SSG office for safekeeping.

My item isn't on the list. What now?

New finds are posted as soon as they're processed, so check back daily. If it still doesn't appear after a few days, contact the team — it may have been logged under a different name or category.

What happens after I submit a claim?

The SSG and EagleCatch team review claims within 24 hours. If your description matches, you'll be contacted at the email you provided to verify ownership and arrange pickup.

I found an item — can I just keep it?

Please don't. Found property should be turned in so it can be returned to its owner. Submit a report and the team will handle the rest.

Someone else claimed "my" item. What do I do?

Claims are verified before release, but mistakes can happen. Contact the team with the item's ASKI ID and your proof of ownership and they'll look into it.
