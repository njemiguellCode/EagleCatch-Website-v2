About us

Built by ASKIans, for ASKIans.

EagleCatch turns the school's lost & found box into a searchable, transparent system that anyone on campus can use.

Our vision

A campus where nothing valuable stays lost — where honesty is the fastest route back to its owner.

Our mission

To give every ASKI student a simple, dependable way to report, search and reclaim lost belongings.
